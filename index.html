01 / PROJECT OVERVIEW

Turning footsteps
into an electrical signal.

A compact engineering exploration of energy harvesting, combining mechanical design, piezoelectric sensing and embedded monitoring.

THE PROJECT

What is Magic Step?

Magic Step investigates the use of piezoelectric discs to convert pressure-induced mechanical deformation into an electrical response. The prototype combines a mechanical platform with an electronic measurement and monitoring concept.

The project documentation records the design process, components, fabrication, electronics integration, prototype development and test observations. Results will be added from actual measurements after testing.

PROJECT OBJECTIVES
  1. 01Explore piezoelectric energy conversion from footsteps.
  2. 02Design a compact mechanical platform and sensor layout.
  3. 03Develop an ESP32-based monitoring interface.
  4. 04Document fabrication, integration and measured testing.

02 / PROBLEM & SOLUTION

Energy is present
in everyday movement.

The project explores whether small amounts of mechanical energy from repeated footsteps can be captured and monitored through a piezoelectric system.

THE CHALLENGE

Mechanical energy is usually dissipated.

Footsteps apply repeated mechanical loads to a surface. Magic Step studies a way to convert part of this input into an electrical signal using piezoelectric elements.

→
THE APPROACH

Capture, condition and monitor.

A piezoelectric sensor array feeds an electrical conditioning stage. The ESP32-based system is intended to monitor step activity and selected electrical or battery-status signals.
