# Magic Step — Project Overview

**Group 16 · Department of Mechanical Engineering · Sanjivani University, Kopargaon**

## Project summary

Magic Step explores converting mechanical energy from footsteps into electrical energy using six piezoelectric discs mounted on a wooden platform. The electronics are documented as a separate assembly. SolidWorks models, project prototype photographs, and Tinkerers Lab manufacturing photographs record the design and learning process.

Electrical output, charging behavior, and battery performance have not been independently validated. The website distinguishes the intended functional sequence from a verified electrical circuit and does not report unmeasured results.

## Problem and proposed approach

Conventional walking surfaces do not utilize the mechanical energy generated by human footsteps. This project explores whether piezoelectric sensors can convert a portion of that pressure into electrical output for potential low-power use.

The documented concept lists six piezoelectric discs, four rectifier diodes, a 50 µF capacitor, a boost converter, a TP4056 charging module, one 3.7 V lithium-ion battery and holder, a 400-point mini breadboard, jumper wires, and an ESP32 development board. The ESP32 is identified separately as a monitoring and control unit where implemented.

## Functional energy-conversion concept

Footstep pressure → piezoelectric energy generation → bridge rectification → energy storage / conditioning → charging module → lithium-ion battery.

The detailed architecture identifies the listed capacitor and boost converter in the functional sequence.
