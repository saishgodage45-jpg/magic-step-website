# Objectives, Scope, and Limitations

**Magic Step · Group 16 · Sanjivani University**

## Objectives

- Explore piezoelectric energy conversion under footstep pressure.
- Document a wooden platform carrying six piezoelectric discs.
- Record the intended rectification, conditioning, and storage blocks without inventing electrical connections.
- Use CAD and prototype-development records to communicate mechanical design and integration.
- Document digital manufacturing learning at Tinkerers Lab.
- Identify safety checks and measurements needed before stating performance results.

## Scope

The project documentation covers the mechanical concept, listed electronics, SolidWorks component modelling, prototype photographs, and separate Makerspace learning activities. The functional flow describes intended system blocks only.

The Bambu Lab A1 printer, Benchy test model, laser machine, and laser-engraved SAISH acrylic nameplate are documented as Tinkerers Lab experience and outputs. They are not represented as Magic Step prototype components.

## Current limitations

- No verified wire-level circuit diagram or ESP32 pin map is available.
- Electrical output, stored energy, charging behavior, and battery performance are not reported as measured results.
- Compatibility between the boost converter, TP4056 module, and specific lithium-ion cell is unverified.
- Cell protection requirements must be reviewed before charging.
- CAD-record dimensions and model details require comparison with the physical prototype.
- No prices were provided for the bill of materials.

## Future scope

With supervisor approval, future work may verify the circuit and charging path, define a repeatable measurement procedure, record test conditions and results, confirm physical dimensions, and document only ESP32 behavior demonstrated on the prototype.
