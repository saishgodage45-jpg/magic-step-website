# Safety and Maintenance

**Magic Step · Group 16 · Sanjivani University**

## Electrical and battery review

- Do not connect or charge the lithium-ion cell until a supervisor has reviewed the charging input, boost-converter output, TP4056 module requirements, cell compatibility, and protection provisions.
- A TP4056 module name alone does not establish that a complete charging or protection circuit is present or suitable for this application.
- Do not infer circuit wiring from the functional energy-flow diagram.
- Use only a supervised, reviewed test procedure and suitable measurement equipment. Document actual test conditions and results.

## Mechanical inspection

- Inspect the wooden platform and sensor mounting before supervised testing.
- Check piezoelectric discs for visible damage and ensure the platform does not apply unintended point loads.
- Inspect wires and soldered connections for strain or damage before each test.
- Stop testing and consult the supervisor if a component is damaged, becomes hot, or behaves unexpectedly.
