## Separate Tinkerers Lab activities

The website's manufacturing gallery documents additional hands-on learning at Tinkerers Lab, Sanjivani University:

- Bambu Lab A1 FDM printer and a Benchy test model.
- Laser cutting machine and a laser-engraved SAISH acrylic nameplate.

These are Makerspace learning activities and outputs. The documentation does not claim that they were used to manufacture the Magic Step wooden platform, sensor mounts, or other prototype components.

## Digital manufacturing workflow

**CAD model → Design preparation → Machine setup → Manufacturing → Final product**

For FDM printing, the learning workflow includes model and STL preparation, slicing, filament loading, printer setup, layer-by-layer printing, and inspection of the finished part. For laser processing, it includes preparing digital geometry, positioning suitable material, supervised machine setup, and cutting or engraving. Machine operation must follow Tinkerers Lab safety procedures and supervision.
